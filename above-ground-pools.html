Included Equipment

Complete Plug & Play System — Nothing Extra to Buy

Every Aquashark above-ground pool ships as a complete, fully-equipped package. Every component is factory-selected and installed — your pool is swim-ready from day one.

  • Skimmer

    Surface debris removal system, pre-fitted into the pool shell.

  • Directional Inlet Nozzles

    Adjustable return jets for optimal water circulation throughout the pool.

  • High-Efficiency Filter

    Cartridge or sand filter system pre-selected to your pool's water volume.

  • Circulation Pump

    Energy-efficient pump sized for your pool — keeps water clean and clear.

  • Complete Plumbing

    All suction and return lines, valves, and fittings pre-assembled for rapid connection.

  • Electrical Control Panel

    On/off electrical panel for pump and lighting — easy one-touch operation.

  • LED Underwater Light

    Waterproof LED pool light factory-fitted into the shell — ready for evening ambience from day one.

  • Installation & Commissioning

    Certified installation team sets up the pool, connects all equipment and runs a full water test at handover.

Installation Process

From order to swim-ready in just days
