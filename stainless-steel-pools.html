Overview

Why stainless steel?

Stainless steel pools offer unmatched durability, hygiene and design flexibility for projects where fiberglass or concrete options are not viable.

Corrosion Resistant

316L marine-grade stainless withstands pool chemicals, salt water and outdoor exposure indefinitely.

Lightweight Structure

Significantly lighter than concrete, making it the preferred choice for rooftop and elevated installations.

Hygienic Surface

Non-porous stainless steel resists biofilm and algae growth, ensuring superior water hygiene.

Long Lifespan

Engineered for decades of continuous use with minimal maintenance requirements.
