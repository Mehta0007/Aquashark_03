Material Advantage

Why stainless steel outperforms every alternative

For rooftop, elevated, commercial or architecturally demanding projects, stainless steel delivers what concrete and fiberglass cannot.

Corrosion Resistant

316L marine-grade stainless withstands pool chemicals, salt water and harsh outdoor environments indefinitely.

Lightweight Structure

Significantly lighter than concrete, making it the preferred choice for rooftop, podium and elevated slab installations.

Superior Hygiene

Non-porous stainless resists biofilm, algae and bacteria — delivering better water quality with lower chemical usage.

Decades of Lifespan

Engineered for permanent installations requiring minimal maintenance across 30+ year project lifespans.

Any Shape or Size

Modular panel system enables any custom shape — round, L-shaped, lap lanes, infinity edges and multi-depth configurations.

Premium Aesthetics

Mirror or brushed finish options. Interior can be fitted with mosaic, epoxy or bare stainless to match any architectural vision.

Rapid Fabrication

Factory-precision manufacturing with modular panels cuts site installation time significantly versus cast concrete.

Fully Recyclable

100% recyclable material at end of life — the most environmentally responsible choice for permanent aquatic facilities.

Material & Engineering

316L Stainless Steel — The Gold Standard

Every Natare pool system is built from 316L austenitic stainless steel — the highest grade used in marine and aquatic applications. The 'L' designation (low carbon) ensures exceptional resistance to sensitisation and inter-granular corrosion, critical for permanent immersion in chemically treated water.

Panels are precision CNC-formed and TIG-welded at controlled tolerances, then passivated for maximum corrosion resistance before dispatch. Every joint is watertight by design — no sealants or coatings are required.
