FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often — straight from our installation and aftersales teams.

How long does a fiberglass pool installation take?

Most fiberglass pools can be installed in as little as 1–3 days, depending on site conditions and the size of the pool. This is significantly faster than concrete construction and causes far less disruption to your property.

What warranty do your pools come with?

All Aquashark fiberglass pools carry a 15-year structural warranty. Hot tubs and swim spas from Platinum Spas and Master Spas come with their respective manufacturer warranties — contact us for full details on each product line.

Do you deliver and install outside Pune?

Absolutely. We operate an authorised dealer network spanning 23 cities across India and also serve clients in the Middle East and Asia-Pacific regions. Get in touch to find your nearest dealer and discuss logistics.

Can I get a fully custom pool design?

Yes — every project is unique to us. We offer fully customised pool designs for residential and commercial clients. Our team works with you from concept through to completion to deliver exactly what you envision, within your space and budget.

What after-sales and maintenance support do you offer?

Our relationship doesn't end at installation. Aquashark provides complete after-sales support including hands-on maintenance guidance, chemical management advice, spare parts supply and comprehensive annual service packages tailored to your product.
