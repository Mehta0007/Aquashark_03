FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a fiberglass pool installation take?

Most fiberglass pools can be installed in 1–3 days depending on site conditions and pool size.

What warranty do your pools come with?

All fiberglass pools come with a 15-year structural warranty. Hot tubs and swim spas carry manufacturer warranties.

Do you deliver outside of Pune and Goa?

Yes, we have an authorised dealer network across 23 cities in India and serve clients in the Middle East and Asia-Pacific.

Can I get a custom pool design?

Absolutely. We offer fully customised solutions for residential and commercial projects. Contact us to discuss your requirements.

How do I maintain my pool or hot tub?

Our team provides complete after-sales support including maintenance guidance and annual service packages.
