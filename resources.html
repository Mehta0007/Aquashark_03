FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a fiberglass pool installation take?

Most fiberglass pools can be installed in as little as 1–3 days, depending on site conditions and the size of the pool. This is significantly faster than concrete construction.

What warranty do your pools come with?

All Aquashark fiberglass pools carry a 15-year structural warranty. Hot tubs and swim spas from Platinum Spas and Master Spas come with their respective manufacturer warranties — contact us for full details.

Do you deliver outside Pune and Goa?

Absolutely. We operate an authorised dealer network spanning 23 cities across India and also serve clients in the Middle East and Asia-Pacific regions. Get in touch to find your nearest dealer.

Can I get a custom pool design?

Yes — every project is unique. We offer fully customised pool designs for residential and commercial clients. Our team works with you from concept to completion to deliver exactly what you envision.

How do I maintain my pool or hot tub?

Our relationship doesn't end at installation. Aquashark provides complete after-sales support including hands-on maintenance guidance, chemical management advice and comprehensive annual service packages.
