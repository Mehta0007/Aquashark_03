The Problem

Hard Water is Damaging Your Pool — and Your Investment

Hard water causes calcium carbonate to precipitate as hard scale. In swimming pool systems, this leads to:

  • Unsightly white deposits on tiles and pool surfaces
  • Reduced heat exchanger and system efficiency
  • Blocked pipework and fittings
  • Increased cleaning, maintenance, and downtime
  • Higher operating and replacement costs
  • Increased electricity consumption
  • Reduced equipment lifespan

Pooltron prevents this problem before it starts.

About the Technology

What is Pooltron®?

Pooltron is a patented non-chemical water conditioning system developed and manufactured in the United Kingdom. Instead of adding chemicals into your water, Pooltron changes the behaviour of dissolved calcium minerals so they remain suspended in water instead of sticking to pool surfaces and equipment. This helps prevent scale formation while maintaining normal water chemistry.

The Science

How Pooltron Works

A simple, elegant 3-step process that protects your pool around the clock.

1

Water Flows Through Pooltron

Your pool's circulation water passes through the Pooltron unit installed inline in the pump room — no bypass, no bypass valves needed.

2

Patented Chamber Changes Crystal Formation

The patented internal chamber changes the crystal structure of dissolved calcium and magnesium minerals without removing them from the water.

3

Minerals Stay Suspended, Not Stuck

Minerals remain suspended in water instead of sticking to surfaces, helping prevent scale formation and keeping systems operating efficiently.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Pooltron soften water?
No. Pooltron prevents scale formation without removing minerals from the water. The mineral content remains unchanged — only the crystal structure is modified so minerals stay suspended rather than depositing on surfaces.
Does it replace chlorine or pool sanitation?
No. Normal pool sanitation remains completely unchanged. Pooltron addresses scale formation only — your chlorine, pH and sanitisation routine continues as normal.
Does it require electricity?
No. Pooltron requires no electrical connection whatsoever. It works purely through water flow and the patented chamber design — completely passive operation.
Does it require any maintenance?
No. Once installed, Pooltron requires zero maintenance, zero servicing, and has no consumable parts. It operates continuously for decades without any intervention.
Will it work with saltwater pools?
Yes. Pooltron is fully compatible with saltwater, chlorinated, and other pool sanitation systems. It works effectively regardless of the sanitisation method used.
How long does it last?
Pooltron is built to last for decades. There are no moving parts, no electronics, and no consumables — just robust stainless steel construction with a proven 50+ year track record.
Is it suitable for fiberglass pools?
Absolutely — and in fact, fiberglass pools benefit significantly. Calcium scaling can permanently affect the gel coat finish of a fiberglass pool, leaving white stains that are very difficult to remove. Pooltron prevents this from happening in the first place.
How is it installed?
Pooltron installs inline in the circulation pipework — typically after the sand filter in the pump room. No power supply, no programming, no structural changes. A qualified plumber can install it in under an hour in most setups.
