Who We Are

India’s Leading Fiberglass Pool Manufacturer

Aquashark is India’s leading fiberglass pool manufacturer and exporter, with authorised dealers across 23 cities in India and 4 overseas locations. We specialise in delivering high-performance pools and wellness systems backed by engineering precision, global partnerships and strong after-sales support.

We are the exclusive distributor for the complete product range of Bullfrog Swim Spas (USA), Chilly Goat Tubs (USA) and Sweaty Goat Saunas across India and the Middle East.

Our Network

Strong Dealer Network & Reliable Support

We supply and install fiberglass pools through our authorised dealer network, consisting of experienced swimming pool industry professionals.

This ensures consistent quality, faster installations and dependable after-sales service, allowing us to support customers efficiently across India and international markets.

Our Expertise

Built on Experience, Driven by Excellence

With over 45 years of collective industry experience in swimming pool and fiberglass manufacturing, our team of engineers and design specialists is committed to building world-class pools tailored to modern lifestyles.
