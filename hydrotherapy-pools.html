Overview

The science of water therapy

Hydrotherapy harnesses the physical properties of water — buoyancy, resistance and temperature — to facilitate rehabilitation, pain management and performance enhancement.

Reduced Joint Load

Buoyancy reduces stress on joints during movement, enabling rehabilitation that is impossible on land.

Resistance Training

Water resistance builds strength without impact, ideal for post-injury and post-surgery recovery.

Temperature Control

Precise thermal regulation for therapeutic outcomes — from warm relaxation to cold contrast therapy.

Pain Management

Hydrotherapy reduces chronic pain and inflammation, supporting long-term patient wellbeing.
