Buyer's guide

How to choose the right hot tub

Four decisions drive the right fit. Our consultants walk you through each during your site survey — here's the short version.

01

Capacity

Count the adults who'll use it regularly, not occasionally. 3-person tubs suit couples; 5–6 person tubs suit families and entertainers.

02

Jets

More jets ≠ better. 40–55 jets is ideal for most. 80+ jet tubs like Onyx or Palma suit serious hydrotherapy and post-workout recovery users.

03

Electrical supply

32 Amp hardwired is standard. 13 Amp plug-and-play (Tahiti) fits apartments and existing sockets — lower heating speed is the trade-off.

04

Space & access

Measure the delivery path, not just the install spot. A 2.6m Maximus needs clearance through gates, stairs and lifts — our team surveys free of charge.

FAQ

Questions we hear every week

How long does delivery and installation take?

Pan-India delivery typically takes 2–4 weeks from order confirmation, depending on model availability and your city. Installation itself is a 1-day job for most models once site prep is complete — our team handles the site survey, electrical coordination and commissioning.

Do hot tubs work in Indian summers?

Yes. All Platinum Spas models have programmable temperature control between roughly 5°C and 40°C, so you can run them cooler during Indian summers as a chill pool, and warmer in winter or monsoon as classic hydrotherapy. Premium Shield insulation also keeps running costs down year-round.

What electrical supply do I need?

Most Platinum hot tubs need a 32 Amp hardwired connection with an RCD-protected dedicated circuit. The Tahiti model runs on a standard 13 Amp plug-and-play socket, which makes it suitable for apartments and retrofits. Our site survey confirms requirements before installation.

What's the running cost in India?

Running costs depend on usage, climate and insulation tier. Platinum Premium Shield insulation (standard on all models) is engineered for thermal efficiency — typical monthly electricity for a well-insulated tub in regular family use runs in a modest band. Exact estimates are provided with your quote based on model and city.

Is the warranty honoured across India?

Yes. Aquashark is the authorized India distributor — warranty claims are handled directly by our service network across 23 cities plus 4 overseas locations. No routing through overseas support.

Can I install a hot tub on my terrace?

Often yes, but terrace installation depends on structural load capacity, drainage, electrical access and access path. A filled tub with people inside can weigh 1,500+ kg — our site survey includes a structural check and will flag if reinforcement is needed.

What's the difference between Deluxe and Premium ranges?

Deluxe models prioritise everyday family use with standard shell depths (870–900mm) and typical jet counts. Premium models feature deeper 940mm shells, more advanced jet distribution, often higher-capacity pumps, and Platinum's top-tier build — suited to serious hydrotherapy and design-led installations.

Do you ship to other countries?

Yes. In addition to 23 Indian cities, Aquashark serves the UAE, Qatar, Oman and Saudi Arabia. WhatsApp us for international delivery, customs and installation coordination.
